# Graph

The map's graph in the palette. A cluster is a disc in `surface` with a `line-strong` hairline, its count in `ink` and its name under it in `muted`; the share that has settled is a thin `done-mark` arc around it. A cluster where something waits on you takes `waiting-fill` with a `waiting-mark` hairline. A stepped-out item is a small disc in its state's fill with a hairline in its mark: moved purple, waiting amber, settled green; an open loop is a dashed `loop-mark` ring with no fill. Edges and tethers are `edge`; a cascade is a `moved-mark` arrow.

No strong colour fills an area or draws a thick ring: fills are pastel, lines are hairlines, and the only thick stroke is an edge, in sand.

A session that waits for a pull request, as Kiro Remote's change feed says, is an edge with an arrow to what it waits for, drawn as a `waiting-mark` line at the weight of a cascade. It goes when the wait ends. The legend names it, so no meaning rests on colour alone.
