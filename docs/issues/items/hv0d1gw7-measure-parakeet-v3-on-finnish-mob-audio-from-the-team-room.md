# Measure Parakeet v3 on Finnish mob audio from the team room

## Goal
Know how well the chosen model transcribes a real Finnish and English mob before building on it.

## Notes
- Open question in the doc (Speech). Record a real session in the team room, with consent, ideally both with a laptop mic and a conference speakerphone, and transcribe it with the helper's pipeline.
- The doc notes that a speakerphone or boundary mic beats a laptop mic by a wide margin.

## Done when
- Word error rate per language and per mic, and the cases that fail, are written down in the issue, with the decision to build on Parakeet v3 or not.
