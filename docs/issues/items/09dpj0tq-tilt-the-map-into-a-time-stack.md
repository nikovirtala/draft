# Tilt the map into a time stack

## Goal
Tilted, the map stacks each day of the span as a layer, with lit items as lines through the layers and a causal trace as a thread through them.

## Notes
- Design: docs/model.md, "The time stack"; spec's planned stack (tilt 0 to 70°, turn within ±60°, Home and H).
- Only what the lens lights stands up; the rest is a faint floor.

## Done when
- An item's line shows its state on each day of its life, tested against the fold of each day.
- The trace of the 23 November cascade is one thread through the right layers.
- The camera can't leave its limits, Home returns flat, and the keyboard does everything the pointer does.
- Frames per second while tilting on the 5,000-item synthetic context are measured and at least 50.
